Berkeley Students' Top 5 Places for Coffee on Campus

For coffee enthusiasts navigating UC Berkeley's campus, finding the perfect brew can feel daunting. Spoon University compiled recommendations from student contributors highlighting the best coffee destinations.

Equator Coffee and Teas (ASUC Student Union)
Located in the newly opened Student Union, Equator features Nitro Cold Brew — a smooth, bubbly alternative to traditional iced coffee with more caffeine than the average iced coffee. The drink maintains creaminess even without additions. Dining Points are not accepted.

Babette (South Hall)
This semester's newcomer occupies UC Berkeley's historic first building. The miniature café mirrors its Bancroft Avenue counterpart, offering espresso drinks and pastries ideal for pre-class stops. Dining Points unavailable.

Qualcomm Cafe (Sutardja Dai Hall)
Serving Peet's Coffee alongside custom wraps, this spot combines meals with caffeine fixes. Abundant seating and a peaceful setting make it suitable for extended study sessions. Dining Points accepted.

Yali's (Stanley Hall)
Daily queues testify to quality lattes accompanied by impressive latte art. The menu extends to salads, sandwiches, and quiches, though focaccia availability diminishes by evening. Dining Points not accepted.

The Coffee Lab (Hildebrand Hall-Chemistry Complex)
Hidden beneath the chemistry building, this gem serves elevated coffee drinks and sandwiches before morning lectures. Limited menu, convenient location. Dining Points not accepted.

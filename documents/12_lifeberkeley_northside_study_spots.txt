Northside Study Spots

The Berkeley campus is surrounded by a variety of cafes that provide study ambience for all your needs: quiet places, social hubs, and everything in between. Away from the hustle and bustle of downtown Berkeley, the city's Northside is home to dozens of cafes and restaurants that are great places to sit down, enjoy a bite, and get some work done.

On Euclid Avenue

Delah Coffee: This eye-catching cafe features Arabian coffee for less than $5, a tiled ceiling, comfy velvet chairs, and barstool seating between outlets. The establishment offers quiet music that is always playing in the background and delivers excellent study vibes with its dark tones and glittering silver chandeliers.

Mind Coffee: Known for its menu of classy signature beverages, this small aesthetic corner features beanbag cushions and regular chairs. The shop has numerous outlets along the walls, its own WiFi, and tasteful, upbeat music. Students can enjoy a matcha latte while taking advantage of the pastry happy hour.

On Hearst Avenue

Foothill Dining Commons: Tucked away on the northeastern corner of campus, this dining commons is surrounded by nature with outdoor seating near the entrance and a TV in the main dining room. Students can use meal swipes or flex dollars to access buffet-style breakfast, lunch, or dinner during operating hours.

V&A Cafe: Located between Soda Hall and Etcheverry Hall, just north of campus, this small cafe serves pastries and offers both indoor wooden seating and umbrella-covered picnic tables outside. It's always filled with students, so you'll never lack company while you work.

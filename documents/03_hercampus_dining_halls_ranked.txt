UC Berkeley Dining Halls Ranked

These rankings are based on the criteria of consistency, convenience, uniqueness, variety, and food quality. Rankings are subject to change throughout the semester.

1. Crossroads
Crossroads ranks first for its reliable food quality and variety. The dining hall offers unique items like Korean Fried Chicken and waffles from the waffle maker not found elsewhere. For southside residents, the location provides excellent convenience. The establishment maintains strong consistency across most dining days.

2. Foothill
Despite its distance from the southside campus, Foothill earns the second position. A distinguishing feature is their pre-made wrapped sandwiches available to-go, a service other dining halls lack. The food quality remains consistently good across multiple visits.

3. Cafe 3
Cafe 3 benefits from convenient proximity but lacks the variety of other facilities. The dining hall presents challenges for students with food allergies seeking appropriate meal options. The line is often very long, so it's hard to get seconds. Portion sizes can leave diners unsatisfied.

4. Clark Kerr
Clark Kerr's location at the farthest campus distance reduces its convenience ranking. The dining hall offers fried foods daily, which some patrons avoid for health reasons. Menu inaccuracies occur frequently, with advertised dishes sometimes replaced with alternatives. Despite these drawbacks, the food quality itself remains acceptable.

The author acknowledges each dining hall experiences fluctuating quality and expresses gratitude toward staff efforts during challenging circumstances.

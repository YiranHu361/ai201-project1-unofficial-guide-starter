UC Berkeley Dining FAQ (2025-2026)

What are my options as a UC Berkeley student for meal plans?
On-campus residents receive the Blue & Gold Plan as part of their housing contract, combining meal swipes, flex dollars, and flex+ dollars. You may upgrade or modify through the official change form during designated periods by contacting Berkeley Housing at reshall@berkeley.edu. Off-campus undergraduates and graduate/extension students can purchase semester-based plans: Standard, Premium, or Platinum (flex/flex+ dollars only, declining balance), or Advantage, Blue & Gold, and Ultimate plans (combining swipes with flex currencies). Add-on flex+ dollars are available anytime at $100 for 100 dollars or $50 for 50 dollars.

When do student meal plans expire?
On-campus meal plans expire at the end of the academic year. Off-campus plans expire at semester's end.

How do meal swipes work?
Meal swipes are stored on your Cal 1 Card and are deducted each time you visit the dining commons or participating locations offering meal equivalency swipes. Weekly swipes expire Saturdays at midnight and reset.

How do flex and flex+ dollars work?
Flex and flex+ dollars are stored on your Cal 1 Card, and are deducted each time you visit a Berkeley Dining location. Flex dollars work at all UC Berkeley Dining locations including Grubhub pickup. Flex+ dollars additionally work on Grubhub nationwide.

Do my flex dollars roll over?
On-campus plans: yes, your flex and flex+ dollars will roll over from the fall semester to the spring semester. Off-campus: only if you purchase a spring plan. Neither rolls to summer or the following academic year; unused funds forfeit by spring semester's end.

Can I pay for guests using my meal plan?
Yes, flex dollars cover guest meals across all student meal plan types.

Can I change my meal plan?
On-campus residents must submit the official change form during designated periods. Upgrading from Blue & Gold to Ultimate costs $475 per semester ($950 annually, non-prorated). Off-campus and graduate students submit the off-campus form during official periods. Cancellations forfeit unused flex/flex+ dollars, incur a $75 fee, and receive prorated refunds.

Can I cancel my meal plan?
On-campus residents cannot cancel — UC Berkeley has a combined room and board program and does not separate the cost of the meal plan from room fees. Off-campus students may cancel through the official form with a $75 fee and prorated refund.

Do you have vegan and vegetarian options?
Berkeley Dining offers a variety of daily selections to meet your vegetarian and vegan needs at all of our locations. Filter online menus by dietary preference; staff can assist with selections.

Do you offer kosher and halal options?
Kosher and halal options are available in all the dining commons upon request, and dedicated stations are available at Café 3. Packaged kosher sandwiches available in convenience stores.

How do you handle food allergies?
Contact the registered dietitian at dietitian@berkeley.edu in advance for one-on-one support. Complete the Food Allergy and Special Diet Notification Form. If you're prescribed medications or epinephrine injectors for food allergies, please carry them with you at all times. Berkeley Dining cannot guarantee allergen identification due to volume and vendor changes; cross-contact possible.

Can I take a few items to-go after getting a meal?
All-you-can-eat commons permit taking fruit or small desserts like cookies, but additional carry-out is not permitted.

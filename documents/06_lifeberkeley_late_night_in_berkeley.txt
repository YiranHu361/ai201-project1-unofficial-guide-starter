Late Night in Berkeley

Where to Study
Multiple Berkeley libraries maintain extended hours for after-hours study sessions:
- Mathematics Statistics Library and Music Library close at 8 p.m.
- Law Library, Bioscience Library, and Doe Library close at 9 p.m.
- Business Library, Chemistry and Chemical Engineering Library, and Environmental Design close at 10 p.m.
- Main Stacks remain open until 2 a.m.
- Moffitt Library operates 24 hours (Note: closing for renovations beginning January 2025)

Late-night cafes include Cafe Milano open until 10 p.m., Victory Point Cafe until 11 p.m., and Caffe Strada until midnight.

Where to Grab a Bite
The Durant Food Court on Durant Avenue features Thai Basil, Kingpin Donuts, and L'Gusto Mex Fusion. Nearby options include IB's, Bunz, Top Dog, La Burrita, and Artichoke's Pizza.
Telegraph Avenue offers Poke Parlor and Tacos Sinaloa, with dessert spots including CREAM, Yogurt Park, Uji Time Dessert, and Little Gem Belgian Waffles.
Downtown areas feature East Bay Spice Company and Eureka.
On-campus dining through Berkeley Dining maintains specific operating hours available through their website.

Evening Activities
Venues like Zellerbach Hall, Wheeler Auditorium, UC Theatre, Berkeley Playhouse, and Greek Theatre host late-night performances.
Recreation options include hiking fire trails and Tilden Park at sunset, plus climbing at Mosaic Boulders gym on Telegraph and the RSF facility open until 11 p.m.

Safety Services
Safewalk provides free walking escorts from dusk until 2:30 a.m. via call 510-642-9255 (642-WALK) or download their Downtowner app.
Night Safety Shuttle operates from 7:30 p.m. to 3 a.m., with door-to-door service from 3 to 5:30 a.m.
